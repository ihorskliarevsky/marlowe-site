Marlowe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Marlowe does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Apple Music

Marlowe asks for access to Apple Music to play tracks and to check that you have a subscription. This happens on your iPhone through Apple. Marlowe does not send your listening or library to us.

Data on your iPhone

Marlowe keeps a playback log on your iPhone (times, track IDs and player events; no name or email). It stays on your phone. We see it only if you choose to share it with us. Marlowe also remembers your settings, on your phone only.

TestFlight

If you test Marlowe through TestFlight, Apple shares your feedback, screenshots and crash reports with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. We use them only to fix and improve Marlowe.

Children

Marlowe is not made for children under 13.
